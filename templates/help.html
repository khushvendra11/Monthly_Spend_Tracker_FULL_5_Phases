🆘 Help & Support

Everything you need to use SpendTrack.

How do I add an expense?

Use + Expense and enter amount, category, payment method, description and date.

How does Smart Budget work?

The planner uses your income and current spending. As a simple starting guide it targets 20% savings and up to 80% spending, then shows daily and category guidance.

What is the daily spending guide?

It divides recommended remaining spending by the number of days left in the month.

How do I add a category?

Open Categories, enter a name and press Add Category. Categories are now saved to your account.

How do I add a profile photo?

Open Profile, choose a PNG/JPG/JPEG/WEBP image and save. Keep the image reasonably small for faster loading.

Budget warnings

The dashboard warns you when your saved budget is nearly used or has been reached.
